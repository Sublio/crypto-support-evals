# Keeping Your Account Secure

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA is required for every withdrawal. Orbitra supports two 2FA methods:

- An authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy)
- A hardware security key

SMS-based 2FA is not offered.

## Password resets

After you reset your password, withdrawals are locked for 48 hours. This protects your funds if someone else has gained access to your email.

## What Orbitra will never ask for

Orbitra support will never ask you for:

- Your password
- Your 2FA codes
- Your seed phrase or private keys

Orbitra will never ask you to send funds to any address. Anyone who asks for these is not Orbitra.

## Contacting support

Official support is available only through the in-app chat. Orbitra does not offer phone support and will never call you.

## If you think your account is compromised

Freeze your account immediately from Settings → Security → "Freeze account". Freezing blocks all trading and withdrawals. To unfreeze your account, you must complete identity verification again.
