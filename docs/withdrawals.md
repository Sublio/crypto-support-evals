# Withdrawals

You can withdraw supported crypto assets from Orbitra Exchange to any external wallet address on a supported network.

## Withdrawal fees and minimums

| Asset / network | Fee | Minimum withdrawal |
|---|---|---|
| USDT on TRON (TRC-20) | 1 USDT | 10 USDT |
| USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20) | 6 USDT | 50 USDT |
| SOL on Solana | 0.01 SOL | 0.1 SOL |
| DOT on Polkadot | 0.1 DOT | 2 DOT |
| ETH on Linea | 0.0005 ETH | 0.005 ETH |

The fee is deducted from the amount you withdraw.

## Processing time

Most withdrawals are processed in under 30 minutes. The first withdrawal to a new address is held for 24 hours as a security measure. After that, withdrawals to the same address are processed normally.

## Network upgrades

Withdrawals on a network are paused during scheduled network upgrades. Upgrades are announced at least 48 hours in advance on the Orbitra status page.

## Sending to the wrong network

Always check that the receiving wallet supports the network you select. Funds sent to the wrong network or to an incorrect address cannot be recovered. Orbitra cannot reverse blockchain transactions.
