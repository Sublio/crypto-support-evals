# Identity Verification (KYC)

Orbitra Exchange is required to verify the identity of its customers. What you can do depends on your verification level.

## Verification levels

| Level | Requirements | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Level 0 | Verified email | View markets only. You cannot trade, deposit or withdraw. |
| Level 1 | ID document and selfie | Trade, deposit, and withdraw up to $5,000 per day |
| Level 2 | Level 1 plus proof of address | Withdraw up to $100,000 per day |

Proof of address must be dated within the last 3 months. Utility bills and bank statements are accepted.

## Accepted ID documents

- Passport
- National ID card
- Driving licence

Residence permits are not accepted for Level 1 verification.

## How long verification takes

Most verifications are completed in about 10 minutes. If your application needs manual review, it can take up to 3 business days.
