# Policies

## No financial advice

Orbitra Exchange does not provide investment, financial or tax advice. Nothing on Orbitra, including content from Orbitra support, is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset. If you need advice, speak to an independent financial adviser.

## Where Orbitra is available

Orbitra is available to residents of the United Kingdom and the European Union, except the Netherlands. Orbitra is not available to residents of the United States.

## Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to open an Orbitra account.

## Inactive accounts

An account is inactive if there has been no login for 24 months. Inactive accounts are charged an inactivity fee of 5 USDT per month, deducted from the account balance. Logging in stops the fee.
