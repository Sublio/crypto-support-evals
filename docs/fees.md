# Trading Fees

Orbitra Exchange charges trading fees based on your 30-day trading volume, measured in USD equivalent. Your tier is recalculated every day at 00:00 UTC.

## Fee tiers

| Tier | 30-day volume | Maker fee | Taker fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Under $50,000 | 0.10% | 0.20% |
| Silver | $50,000 to $500,000 | 0.08% | 0.15% |
| Gold | Over $500,000 | 0.05% | 0.10% |

A **maker** order adds liquidity to the order book (for example, a limit order that does not fill immediately). A **taker** order removes liquidity (for example, a market order).

## ORB holder discount

If you hold at least 1,000 ORB, the Orbitra exchange token, in your account, you get an additional 25% discount on your trading fees. The discount is applied on top of your tier fee. For example, a Silver-tier taker fee of 0.15% becomes 0.1125%.

## Deposits and purchases

- Crypto deposits are free on all supported networks.
- Buying crypto with a debit or credit card costs 3.5% of the purchase amount.
- Bank transfers (SEPA and UK Faster Payments) are free.

## Withdrawal fees

Withdrawal fees depend on the asset and network. See the Withdrawals page for the full list of withdrawal fees and minimums.
